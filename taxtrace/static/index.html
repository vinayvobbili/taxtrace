Drop files or a folder here
PDFs, spreadsheets, photos, or a zip from your preparer's portal. They go to this server on your computer and nowhere else; nothing is filed until you press the button below.

A document's form, year and owner come from its name and the folder it sat in, the same way taxtrace import sorts them. Filed returns go to prior-returns/, the rest to a folder for their tax year. Identity documents (driver's licenses, Social Security cards, passports) and copies already imported are skipped. Owner is a name from redact-terms.txt found in the file's name.
