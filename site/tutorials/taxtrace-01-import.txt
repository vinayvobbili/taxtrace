A preparer's portal hands you a pile of files. There's a passport in it, forms named whatever, and some of them twice.

Every number, traced to its page.

Open the Import tab and choose the files your preparer sent. They go to taxtrace on your own computer, and nowhere else. Nothing is filed yet: the page only reads what each file is called.

For each file, taxtrace shows its form, its tax year and its owner, and where it will go. A filed return goes to prior-returns. A passport is skipped, and so is a copy you already have.

Press the button, and the rest are filed. Back on the Overview, they sit in their year's folder, each tagged with its form, and the older return is with the filed returns.
