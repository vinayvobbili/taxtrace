A preparer's portal hands you a zip. There's a driver's license in it, forms named whatever, and some of them twice.

Every number, traced to its page.

Once your documents are imported, this page shows them sorted by tax year, with filed returns in a folder of their own.

To import, point taxtrace at the folder or the zip. Each file goes to its tax year, last year's filed return goes to prior-returns, and the driver's license is skipped: it's no tax input, and a copy not made can't leak.

Import the same folder again, and nothing changes. Files already in the workspace are skipped.
