Your AI assistant could be a real help with your taxes, but you wouldn't paste your whole tax folder into it.

Every number, traced to its page.

Everything on this page is what your AI assistant can see too, but only in masked form. It connects through M C P, the standard way to plug tools into an assistant.

One command adds taxtrace to Claude Code. The assistant is offered five tools: status, the list of documents, a document's outline, form checks, and the to-do list. Every one is read-only.

What comes back is counts and masked text. The dates are real, but the wording is masked, and no tool returns an amount or a name. A locked document is never opened, and the Keychain is never asked.
