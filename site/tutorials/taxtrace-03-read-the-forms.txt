A number on a form is only useful if you can trust it. Is it from the right box? Did a digit slip? And who has time to check every one by hand?

Every number, traced to its page.

On the page, each document wears a badge for the form it holds: W-2, 1098 or 1099. Reading them is one command each.

Here the W-2 and the mortgage statement are read. Every box is found by the words of its label, so a payroll company's own layout reads too, and each reading says how many of its checks pass.

Each value is saved with its page, its place on it, and the exact text it was read from. A check throws out any value whose text isn't on that page, and the form's own arithmetic is checked: box 4 must be 6.2 percent of box 3.
