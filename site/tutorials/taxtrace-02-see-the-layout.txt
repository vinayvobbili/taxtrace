Sometimes you want help with a form, but you'd rather not show anyone your numbers, your name, or your town.

Every number, traced to its page.

Every document in your workspace is listed here, tagged with the form it holds. To see inside one without its values, taxtrace has two views.

The first is the outline: each page's first lines, so you can see which forms a document holds, and in what order. Names and amounts are already masked.

The second is the redacted layout. It keeps the form's shape, and replaces every digit with a pound sign, and every name, street and town with stars. That is safe to show to whoever is helping you.
