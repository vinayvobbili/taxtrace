Typing a return into tax software means answering question after question, in an order you don't choose, and wondering where each figure was.

Every number, traced to its page.

Start on the page. The to-do list shows what's due, and the moves show what could still lower the tax.

The entry sheet lists everything your tax software will ask, in the order it asks. Under each form, each value shows the document and page it was read from.

Each row is ready, an estimate, or missing. A missing row names the command that fills it, and names, Social Security numbers and bank numbers are left for you to type.
