taxtrace

Every number on your return, traced to its page.

For anyone who does their own US taxes from a pile of PDFs: W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, a rental, a foreign bank account. taxtrace reads them on your own computer and builds the sheet you type into your tax software, with the file, page and exact text behind every value. It never files anything and never guesses: what it can't settle becomes a question for you. Free and open source.

What it does

Read

Reads what you already have

W-2s, 1098s, brokers' 1099s, K-1s and K-3s, a rental's statements, foreign bank statements and last year's filed return. Scanned pages are read on your computer too.

Trace

Every number has a source

Each value keeps the file, the page and the exact text it was read from. A check throws out any value whose quote isn't on that page.

Check

The arithmetic is verified

A K-1's capital account has to add up, a rental's Schedule E has to tie out, last year's carryovers have to roll forward. A miss is shown, not smoothed over.

Ask

Questions instead of guesses

A receipt that could be a repair or an improvement, a missing 1099, a rate to choose: each is a question for you, with the evidence next to it.

Plan

The year, ahead of time

Estimated-tax payments against the safe harbor, tax-saving moves ranked by the dollars each saves, IRA limits, stock sales and wash-sale windows.

Agents

Works with your AI assistant

Ask Claude (or another assistant that supports MCP, the standard way to plug tools into AI chat) what's in your workspace and what's left to do. It only ever gets masked text.

Read the narration

A tax preparer's export lands in a folder. taxtrace import sorts it into the workspace by tax year and leaves out identity documents. The page lists each document and which forms it holds.

Next, the W-2 from Initech. taxtrace w2 reads each box by the words of its label, and every value shows the page and the exact line it came from. Box 4 has to be 6.2 percent of box 3, and it is.

A K-1 from Globex Partners goes through the same way. The capital account adds up, so it passes. A receipt that could be a repair or an improvement becomes a question, with the receipt beside it.

The entry sheet then lists everything your tax software will ask, in the order it asks. Each row is ready, an estimate, or missing, and names the command that fills it.

Last, the chat. Before anything goes to a cloud model, digits become pound signs and names become stars. A question with an amount in it stays on your Mac, and the page shows exactly what was sent.

How it works

Six parts, each with a short video.

1

Import

Point taxtrace import at a folder or a zip, such as a preparer's portal export. Documents are sorted by tax year, filed returns go to their own folder, and identity documents and repeats are skipped.

2

See the layout

taxtrace outline lists which forms each document holds, page by page. taxtrace redact masks a page down to its shape, so a form can be studied without its values, names or towns.

3

Read the forms

Commands such as taxtrace w2, 1099, k1 and 1098 read each form box by box. Every value carries its page and quote, and each form's own arithmetic is checked.

4

Get the entry sheet

taxtrace entry writes everything your tax software will ask, in its order. Each row is ready, an estimate or missing, and the questions still open are gathered at the end. Names, Social Security numbers and bank numbers are left for you to type.

5

Ask in the chat

taxtrace ui opens your workspace in the browser with a chat for tax rules, forms and what a document is for. Under each answer from a cloud model, the page shows exactly what was sent. It can't open a document or file anything.

6

Use it from your AI assistant

taxtrace-mcp lets Claude Code, Claude Desktop or any MCP client see what's in your workspace and what's on your to-do list. Every tool is read-only and returns masked text only, never an amount or a name.

Private by design

Your documents are read on your computer. One optional feature can reach a cloud model, and it only gets text with the values taken out.

  • Your documents never leave your computer. Reading, scanned-page OCR, the checks and the entry sheet all run locally. Your workspace is a plain folder on your disk, and taxtrace refuses to put it inside a git repository.
  • Passwords stay in the Keychain. A locked PDF's password is kept in the macOS Keychain, never in a file or on a command line, and taxtrace keychain setup makes each read ask for your Touch ID or Apple Watch.
  • Where a language model reads your documents, it runs on your Mac. It is a local MLX model, never a public API.
  • The one exception is the optional chat. By default it tries Claude's API, then Google's Antigravity, then a model on your Mac, and a cloud model is used only if you gave it a key or installed it. Cloud models see masked text only: digits become #, and names, streets and towns become *s. A question with an amount in it is answered on your Mac and never sent. Run taxtrace ui --models local to keep the chat on your computer entirely.
  • The MCP server is read-only and masked. A locked document is never opened and the Keychain is never asked.
  • No accounts, no usage data. The only outside lookup is public exchange rates from the Federal Reserve and the Treasury, which carries nothing about you.
