# taxtrace documentation

The [project README](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/README.md) has the short version. These pages hold the detail, one topic each.

- [Install, workspace and documents](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/workspace.md): install, the workspace folder, import, outline, reading scanned pages (OCR), locked PDFs and the macOS Keychain (the Mac's password vault)
- [Commands](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/commands.md): every `taxtrace` command, one line each, linked to its page
- [Carryforwards](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/carryforwards.md): depreciation, passive losses and foreign tax read from filed returns
- [Schedule K-1s](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/k1.md): Form 1065 K-1s, North Carolina's K-1, Schedule K-3
- [Passive activities](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/passive.md): each activity's year and Form 8582
- [Forms](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/forms.md): W-2, 1098, 1099-DIV and 1099-INT, Schedule E, Form 8995
- [Planning](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/planning.md): estimated tax, IRAs, a change of job, stock sales, the year's moves
- [The return](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/return.md): entry sheet, adjusted gross income, total tax, year over year, Form D-400, to-dos
- [Foreign side](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/foreign.md): India's return, bank statements, FBAR, Forms 1116 and 8938
- [Budget](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/budget.md): a household budget against your bank and card exports, and the page's Budget tab
- [Chat](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/chat.md): the browser page, the chat and what a cloud model sees
- [MCP server and agent skill](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/mcp.md): masked, read-only tools for Claude or another assistant
- [Development](https://github.com/vinayvobbili/taxtrace/blob/main/docs/development.md): tests, hooks, the privacy audit
